# Navigation is by click, not by scroll

The site used to be toured by scrolling a nine-screen page, with the camera following the scroll bar along a
fixed path. A recruiter skims for 30 to 60 seconds and wants to go straight to one thing: with scrolling they
don't know how far to go, they pass every station on the way, it is awkward on a phone, and there was no way to
link to one station. So the camera now follows a visit, not the scroll position. The site opens on an overview
of the room; the HUD's station links, the desk meter, the keys on the meter bar, the arrow keys, Esc and a click on a TV move the visitor; a flight goes straight to the
chosen station and passes no other. The wheel never moves the camera, and only scrolls a panel whose text is too
long. Reduced motion gets a quick fade in place of a flight. The shift is unchanged, and starts once the flight
has landed. With no scroll there is nothing to restore after a reload, so the shift's restore window is gone.
The reasons and the full design are in spec #170.
